Your data is stored on your PC

  • Your history, library, prompts and documents are stored on your PC. If you turn on Sync, encrypted copies also go to a sync folder you choose. There’s no sAInmore account and no sAInmore server.
  • No telemetry, no tracking, no ads.
  • API keys are encrypted by Windows and only sent to the provider they belong to.
  • The app checks GitHub for a new version when it starts and every 6 hours while it runs. Nothing is downloaded or installed until you click Update now. You can turn the automatic check off in Settings → About.
  • Feedback is only sent when you choose to send it.
  • It uses the official AI websites and official APIs, or a server you add yourself. It doesn’t scrape websites or type into them for you; the Summariser reads only a page you ask it to. Other websites open only in the Web, Dev, Gaming or your own workspaces, when you choose them.

When you use an AI service, what you send goes to that service under its own terms. Full privacy notice

What is the simplest way to start learning a new language as an adult? Answer in under 100 words.Ctrl + Enter
View

Answer A · gemini-3.8-flash (Google) · ≈ $0.0028

The simplest way is to build a low-friction daily habit:

  1. Learn the 100 most common words: These make up roughly 50% of spoken conversation. Use free flashcard apps like Anki.
  2. Use a structured app: Spend 10–15 minutes daily on Babbel or Duolingo to absorb basic grammar without getting overwhelmed by textbooks.
  3. Listen to familiar media: Watch a favorite movie dubbed in the new language with subtitles, or listen to slow-speaking learner podcasts.

Consistency beats intensity: 15 focused minutes every single day is vastly more effective than a weekly two-hour cram session.

Answer B · kimi-k3 (Moonshot AI) · ≈ $0.0123

Start with just 10–15 minutes daily—consistency beats marathon sessions. Use a free app like Duolingo for basics and vocabulary. Focus on the most common 500–1,000 words and simple phrases you’d actually use. Surround yourself with the language: switch your phone’s settings, listen to music or podcasts, watch shows with subtitles. Most importantly, start speaking early, even if just to yourself—mistakes are how you learn. Once you have foundations, try language exchange apps like Tandem or HelloTalk to practice with native speakers. Small daily habits compound into real progress.

Answer C · openai/gpt-oss-120b (OpenAI’s open model, run by Groq) · under $0.001

  1. Set a clear, tiny goal – e.g., “learn 10 everyday phrases this week.”
  2. Pick a single resource (app like Duolingo, a podcast, or a textbook) and use it daily for 15‑20 minutes.
  3. Speak immediately – repeat aloud, record yourself, or find a language‑exchange partner on apps like Tandem.
  4. Label your environment – stick sticky notes on objects with the new word.
  5. Stay consistent – make it a habit at the same time each day; consistency beats marathon sessions.

Keep it simple, fun, and repeat daily.

This page is a Wikipedia article providing an overview of photosynthesis, detailing its biological mechanisms, evolutionary history, scientific discovery, and controlling factors.

Process Overview and Function

The article defines photosynthesis as the system of biological processes used by autotrophic organisms—such as plants, algae, and cyanobacteria—to convert light energy into chemical energy stored in organic compounds like sugars. While oxygenic photosynthesis splits water to release oxygen as a byproduct, some bacteria and archaea perform anoxygenic photosynthesis using alternative electron donors like hydrogen sulfide or using proton pumps like bacteriorhodopsin. Photosynthesis captures energy globally at a rate of approximately 130 terawatts and fixes about 100–115 billion tons of carbon into biomass every year.

Cellular Structures and Stages

In plants and algae, photosynthesis takes place in chloroplasts, specifically within flattened disk-like membranes called thylakoids surrounded by an aqueous fluid called stroma. In photosynthetic bacteria, light-harvesting proteins are embedded directly in cellular or intracytoplasmic membranes.

Photosynthesis occurs across two consecutive stages:

  • Light-Dependent Reactions: Chlorophyll and accessory pigments absorb photons, transferring electrons through an electron transport chain (the Z-scheme). This process splits water (photolysis), releases oxygen, and produces ATP and NADPH while generating a chemiosmotic proton gradient. Cyclic reactions produce only ATP, whereas non-cyclic reactions yield both ATP and NADPH.
  • Light-Independent Reactions (Calvin Cycle): Taking place in the stroma, the enzyme RuBisCO captures atmospheric carbon dioxide and incorporates it into ribulose 1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP). Utilizing ATP and NADPH, this pathway generates three-carbon sugars (glyceraldehyde 3-phosphate) to form carbohydrates such as glucose, sucrose, and starch.

Carbon Concentrating Mechanisms

Plants have evolved alternate adaptations to optimize carbon fixation:

  • C4 Fixation: Plants like maize and sugarcane separate primary carbon fixation by PEP carboxylase in mesophyll cells from the Calvin cycle in bundle sheath cells, reducing photorespiration in hot environments.
  • CAM Photosynthesis: Succulents and cacti capture CO₂ at night using PEP carboxylase and store it as malate, releasing it during the day when stomata are closed.
  • Alarm Photosynthesis and Aquatic Adaptations: Certain plants utilize calcium oxalate crystals as emergency CO₂ pools. In water, cyanobacteria utilize proteinaceous carboxysomes, and algae utilize pyrenoids to concentrate CO₂ around RuBisCO.

Efficiency and Evolution

Plant photosynthetic efficiency typically ranges from 0.1% to 8% (usually 3–6%). Energy transfer through pigments is enhanced by "quantum walking," where exciton waves sample multiple pathways to find the quickest route to reaction centers.

Photosynthetic life dates back approximately 3.4 billion years. Endosymbiotic theory indicates that eukaryotic chloroplasts originated when primitive eukaryotic cells engulfed photosynthetic cyanobacteria. Eukaryotic lineages have since diversified into green and red plastid lineages, alongside eight distinct lineages of photosynthetic bacteria.

History of Discovery

Key milestones in photosynthesis research include:

  • Jan Ingenhousz (1779): Discovered that green plants require sunlight to restore depleted air, building on earlier experiments by Jan van Helmont and Joseph Priestley.
  • Cornelis Van Niel: Proved photosynthesis is a light-dependent redox reaction.
  • Robert Emerson and Robert Hill: Identified the two light reactions/photosystems and proved that evolved oxygen originates from water.
  • Melvin Calvin, Andrew Benson, and James Bassham: Elucidated the carbon reduction cycle using carbon-14.

Limiting Factors

Photosynthesis is primarily limited by four environmental factors: light irradiance and wavelength, carbon dioxide concentration, water availability, and temperature. Low CO₂ relative to oxygen prompts RuBisCO to bind oxygen instead, causing photorespiration, which consumes energy and decreases sugar yield.

Questions

Do I need API keys?

No. The AI websites work with your normal sign-ins, including free accounts and subscriptions you already have.

API keys are only needed for Compare, Quick ask, AI Studio, the Translator and Summariser, and writing prompts with AI. You can also use Compare, Write with AI and the Tools with free local models through Ollama or LM Studio, without any key. If Ollama has no models yet, AI providers offers three to download, and shows the space each one needs first.

Does my ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro subscription cover the API?

No. Website subscriptions and API billing are separate. Compare and Quick ask use your own API keys, and each provider bills you directly. sAInmore shows an estimated cost under each answer; your provider’s billing page has the exact amount.

Is it free?

Yes, sAInmore is free during the beta. The only costs are the ones you choose: AI subscriptions and API usage, paid to the providers.

Why does Windows warn me when I run it?

Because the app isn’t code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. More about this.

Why didn’t Update now install the new version?

Windows Smart App Control, or another security setting, can block programs that aren’t code-signed, and sAInmore isn’t signed yet. From 0.8.10 the app tells you when this happens; earlier versions just show “Update failed”. Either way, click Download page and install the new version from there. Your data stays.

Windows Defender says sAInmore contains a threat, or it won’t open after updating

Since 6 November 2026, Windows Defender and Smart App Control block a file that versions 0.8.19 to 0.8.21 ship with: ffmpeg.dll, part of Electron, the engine sAInmore runs on. It’s a false alarm: the file is Electron’s own, unchanged. From 0.8.22 on, sAInmore uses an Electron version that Windows lets through. Install it from the download section; your data and settings stay as they are.

What happened to the portable version?

It’s retired from version 0.8.2, because it couldn’t update itself. If you used it, install with Setup: it uses the same data folder, so everything is still there.

Does sAInmore read or send messages on the AI websites?

No. The websites run in their own isolated windows inside the app. sAInmore doesn’t read what’s on them, type into them or send for you. To ask several models automatically, Compare uses the providers’ official APIs, or a server you add yourself.

Which AI services can I add?

In AI work, only AI services: the eight built-in ones, plus services from a reviewed list in the app. Other websites open in the other workspaces (Web, Dev, Gaming and your own), each with its own tabs and sign-ins.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not at the moment. sAInmore is for Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit.

Wasn’t it called brAInsphere?

Yes, until October 2026. Installing sAInmore over brAInsphere keeps your data, settings and website sign-ins.

How do I report a problem or suggest something?

Use Feedback in the app’s Menu, at the bottom of the sidebar. You decide what to include, and nothing is sent until you choose to send it.
