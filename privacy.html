Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

This notice covers two things: this website, and the sAInmore app for Windows. In short, neither collects your data for us. There’s no sAInmore account, no sAInmore server, no analytics and no advertising.

This website

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. That log is GitHub’s, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We don’t receive or use it.
  • The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or social media widgets.
  • Everything on it, including the fonts, is served from this site. Your browser contacts no other server while you read it.
  • The download link takes you to GitHub, where GitHub’s own terms and privacy statement apply.

The sAInmore app

What’s stored on your PC

  • Your history, library, prompts, assistants, brand kits, workflows, benchmarks, projects, documents and settings are stored on your PC, in your Windows user profile (%APPDATA%). If you turn on Sync, encrypted copies also go to a sync folder you choose (see below).
  • API keys you enter are encrypted by Windows for your user account, and are only sent to the provider they belong to.
  • Spending is counted on your PC for every call, also with History off: tokens and estimated cost per model and day, never what you asked or the answers.
  • Keep a history on this PC (the switch in Settings → Privacy). On: kept so you can search and reopen them: your Compare questions and answers, workflow runs, assistant chats, and Ask all panes’ last 8 questions. Nothing is uploaded, unless Sync is on: Sync also copies the comparisons, runs and chats, encrypted, to your sync folder; Ask all panes’ questions stay on this PC. API keys are never stored in history. Off: nothing new is kept, so a chat can’t be reopened once you close it. What’s already saved stays until you delete it, and Ask all panes’ last questions are cleared. Your Library, prompts, assistants, Internal benchmarks results and spending count carry on as usual. Chats on the AI websites themselves are kept by those sites, whichever you choose.
  • Colours you make are kept in your preferences file, so they aren’t in backups or Sync.
  • When you save a file that’s already in your Library, sAInmore says so and keeps the one you have; nothing is uploaded to check this. Names an AI suggests for your files, and your Listing studio drafts, are kept on this PC only.
  • This PC folders (in the Library): folders you add under This PC are read on this PC only, when the Library shows them, and for a folder you keep in your Library, when a new file arrives there while sAInmore is open (it comes in through the Library’s checks; files already there, online-only files and unfinished downloads are left alone); nothing is uploaded by sAInmore, but once a file is in your Library it is part of it: with Sync on, a file that came in this way goes to your other PCs encrypted, like any Library file (files the Library keeps only on this PC stay there). Video previews are made in a separate window that can reach nothing but that one file. sAInmore writes there only when you copy something out, after asking once for that folder, and never replaces a file. Renaming and deleting there need a second, separate yes; a delete always asks first and goes to the Recycle Bin, never deleted for good. Nothing on your PC is ever opened or run from sAInmore. The list of folders stays on this PC: it isn’t in backups or Sync.
  • Used in and Recent: the Library notes where you used a file (Compare, Workflows, AI Studio, DeepDive, Creator’s Lab) and when, to show Used in and Recent. With Sync on, that note goes to your other PCs with the file.
  • Older versions: when you replace a file in the Library, the older version is kept on this PC so you can restore it. Older versions are in your backups but not in Sync, and Delete older versions removes them.
  • Any kind of file: the Library can keep any kind of file. sAInmore never opens, previews or runs a file it can’t show safely; it keeps it under a name of its own and gives it back marked as downloaded. These files aren’t sent to Sync or to any AI.
  • The Tray: its list of files stays on this PC (not in backups or Sync). Files you drag out of it are copies, made in a temporary folder that’s emptied when you close sAInmore.
  • Social workspace: it has its own sign-ins, kept on this PC apart from your other workspaces and from your browser. They aren’t in backups or Sync.
  • Voice typing and search by meaning run on your PC. Their models are downloaded once, only when you choose to turn those features on.
  • The app has no telemetry or tracking.

When the app connects to the internet

  • AI websites you open in the app work as they would in a browser. What you type there goes to that service, under its own terms and privacy policy. Each service has its own separate sign-in inside the app, the same in every workspace. A site can use your microphone only after you press its own microphone button and allow it for that site; a site that checks quietly isn’t told which microphones you have. You can turn it off again from the site’s toolbar. Camera and screen sharing are always blocked for the websites.
  • Other websites open in the Web, Dev, Gaming and your own workspaces (AI work holds AI services only), and work the same way: what you type there goes to that site, under its own terms. Each workspace keeps its own web sign-ins and cookies on your PC, separate from the AI services: Web keeps the ones it had as Webspace, Dev and Gaming have their own, and a workspace you add can keep its own or share Web’s. Deleting a workspace you added deletes its own web sign-ins; AI sign-ins are never deleted. Only https pages open, except that Dev can also open your own projects on this PC over http (localhost, 127.0.0.1 or [::1]): that page itself is loaded from this PC, but like any web page it can load things from the internet. Words you type in the address box are sent to DuckDuckGo as a search. A web page there can capture the mouse (Esc gives it back); AI websites can’t. Files you download go to your Downloads folder. Check the address bar before you sign in.
  • Compare, AI Studio and Quick ask send your question, and any files you attach, directly from your PC to the providers you choose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Mistral, xAI, DeepSeek, Groq, Moonshot, Perplexity or Xiaomi), using your own API key. Where they keep your data: Mistral in the EU by default (it may use subprocessors outside the EU); DeepSeek in China; Groq in the United States; Moonshot (Kimi) is a Chinese company; Perplexity (USA) doesn’t train on API data and keeps it for a period it doesn’t publish, and the app asks it not to store answers; Xiaomi is a Chinese company, and its MiMo API is run by Xiaomi Technology Netherlands (for the EU, UK and Switzerland) or Xiaomi Technologies Singapore (elsewhere), hosted in the Netherlands and Singapore, and may pass data to affiliates elsewhere. Don’t send anything personal or confidential to DeepSeek, Moonshot or Xiaomi; some employers don’t allow them on work devices. The app shows these notes where you add each key. If you turn on web search, the provider searches the web for you. Local models through Ollama or LM Studio stay on your PC: the app reaches them only on this PC (Ollama at 127.0.0.1:11434, LM Studio at 127.0.0.1:1234). Ollama’s cloud models (names ending in cloud) run on ollama.com.
  • Kept by AI providers: most AI providers keep a processed form of recent requests (not their text) for your account, to make repeats cheaper: OpenAI up to 24 hours, encrypted; Gemini up to 24 hours, in memory; DeepSeek hours to days, on disk; Groq up to 2 hours after last use. xAI, Mistral, Moonshot and Xiaomi don’t say for how long. Perplexity says its API keeps none. Anthropic keeps none unless asked, and sAInmore doesn’t ask. Local models keep nothing outside this PC.
  • Write with AI and Improve with AI (in Prompts) send your task or prompt to the model you pick, only when you press the button. With a local model, it stays on your PC.
  • Servers you add yourself (in OpenAI’s format, such as OpenRouter or a company server): your questions, files and that server’s key go to the address you typed (https, or http only for a server on this PC). sAInmore can’t check what such a server does with your messages, or who runs it, so only add one you trust. For OpenRouter, you can require zero data retention.
  • Combine and the AI judge (in Compare) send your question and the answers to the one model you pick, only when you click. The app says where they go before sending. Gemini answers that used Google Search are left out.
  • Challenge (in Compare), only when you click: Challenge this claim sends the sentence you picked, and your objection, back to the model that wrote it; Challenge… sends each model the other models’ answers, without their names, and asks it to revise its own. Gemini answers that used Google Search are never shown to another company’s model.
  • Workflows (in Compare) send each step’s instruction, the files or passages you give that step, and the answers you pass on, to the models you pick for that step; a second opinion sends the same to one more model you pick. Before each step the app shows what it sends, to which company and the estimated cost, and nothing is sent until you run it. Gemini answers that used Google Search, and anything later Gemini steps write from them, only go on to Gemini models. Workflow runs are kept only while History is on.
  • Internal benchmarks (in Compare) sends each task in your set to the models you pick, only when you run it, after showing the cost. Its checks run on this PC; if you turn on the AI judge, the answers also go to the judge model it names. Past runs stay on this PC; your task sets go to your Sync folder only if Sync is on.
  • External benchmarks (in Compare): Update downloads public leaderboard files from huggingface.co (Arena) and epoch.ai (Epoch AI), only when you press Update or turn on checking when you open it. Nothing about you is sent. The results are kept on this PC.
  • Project planner (in Projects) sends what you describe, and any files you attach, to the model you pick, and the check step to the model it uses, after one OK from you. It shows the cost and where your text goes before you start. With a model on this PC, it stays on your PC.
  • Assistants send your question and the assistant’s instructions to the models it uses, like Compare. The Reflection Journal template is meant for a model on this PC (Ollama or LM Studio), so your entries stay private; if you pick an online model for it instead, your entries go to that provider. An assistant’s files are searched on this PC, and only the matching passages go to the model. Memory is off until you turn it on; its notes come only from your own words, you can edit them, and they go with every message to the chosen models.
  • Creator’s Lab sends your text to the model you pick, only when you ask, and Content Multiplier shows the cost first. Brand DNA kits are kept on this PC and go to your other PCs only with Sync. Check the tone sends a text and the kit’s tone and words to the model you pick. A brand guide is read on this PC, and its text goes to the model you pick after you say yes. Test tone can read a sample aloud through Gemini or Windows’ own voice. The Listing studio sends your photos, made small, and the facts you give to the model you pick, only when you ask, with the cost shown first; sAInmore never posts a listing for you.
  • Social posts sends your brief (what it’s about, the sites, your notes, and any link as text, never opened by sAInmore) to the AI you choose. Drafts are kept on this PC, and with Sync on they go to your other PCs. Nothing is posted for you.
  • Plan & post keeps your calendar on this PC (with Sync on, on your other PCs too) and reminds you with a Windows notification at the time you set. sAInmore never posts: it opens the site, or the site’s own scheduler, for you to post.
  • Titles & Thumbnails keeps the titles, what the video is about and your notes in a results log on this PC; with Sync on, on your other PCs. Its pictures are saved in your Library.
  • Picture edits: Looks, Auto and Retouch work on this PC; nothing is sent. Edit with AI sends the picture, a copy with your circles marked, and your words to the picture AI you choose; only the parts inside your circles are taken from its answer, and the result is saved as a new picture beside the original.
  • Sticker packs sends each expression (with your mascot’s picture, or your description) to the picture AI you choose. The words on the stickers aren’t sent: the cut-out, border, words and checks happen on this PC. Stickers you haven’t saved, and the earlier versions of each, are kept in this app’s data on this PC (never sent, never synced; at most about 120 MB, and you can remove any of them) so a restart doesn’t lose them. sAInmore never adds a pack to an app for you.
  • Design Canvas (in AI Studio) keeps your designs and their versions on this PC; with Sync on, on your other PCs too. Show me 3 directions sends what it’s for, your words, the size and your kit’s colours and font names to the text AI you choose; your pictures aren’t sent. Print files are saved in your Library; sAInmore never uploads them to a print shop.
  • 3D Lab (in AI Studio) opens a model file you pick (GLB, STL or OBJ) and checks it on this PC. The file is read in a separate process, nothing is sent anywhere and nothing is saved or changed; closing the model forgets it.
  • Ask about a file and Name with AI (in the Library) send that file’s picture, made small, or up to 40,000 characters of a document, with its title, kind, source and prompt, to the model you pick, only when you ask and with the cost first.
  • Google Fonts: the app contacts Google Fonts only when you add a Google font, or after you turn on “Show Google font previews” in Brand DNA, which is off at first. Google then sees your IP address and the fonts’ names and, for previews, the letters shown. With previews on, fonts load as they come into view. Added fonts are kept in your Library.
  • Use copied text reads the clipboard only when you press its hotkey, and nothing is sent until you choose.
  • Ask with AI in DeepDive sends the parts of your files that match your question, and the text around them (short documents almost whole), to the model you pick. Lines that try to give AI tools orders are left out. Find text sends nothing.
  • Research in DeepDive sends your topic, questions and filters to the AI searchers you pick, each through its own provider’s web search with your own API key, only when you click and after showing the cost. If you add your files, they’re searched on your PC and the searchers don’t get them. A passage from a file is sent only if you keep it as evidence and ask for key facts, a report or a translation, and then only to the model you pick for that. A Gemini searcher’s Google Search answer is shown as Google returned it, in its own column, and is never used for the evidence board, the key facts or the report.
  • Knowledge (Library → Context) keeps your notes and files on this PC and never syncs them, even with Sync on; they are read into passages and searched on this PC, and searching by words needs no AI. When you turn the Knowledge chip on in Compare, the Assistants, AI Studio or Creator’s Lab and ask, only the passages that match your question (a few thousand characters at most) go to the model you pick, with the question, never a file or a whole collection. A new collection is locked to this PC: a locked collection is never given to a cloud model, an Ollama cloud model included (a model that runs on this PC can still use it). “What was sent” under an answer lists what each model was given, and is kept on this PC for 90 days; removing Knowledge or a collection forgets it. Copy knowledge in the Workspace only copies text for you to paste yourself.
  • Automations run a saved Workflow, or write a short draft or digest, on a schedule or when a new file lands in a folder you chose, on a model you pick, with your own key or a model on this PC. Each time one runs, it sends its task and the new files it reads to the model you chose for it, and posts nothing. Describe it, which turns a sentence you write into the steps of an automation, sends that sentence and the names (never the paths) of the folders you picked to one model, on this PC when you have one, and names that model before you press the button. Every result waits in the Inbox on this PC, and the monthly limit you set in Usage stops them too. “Keep running in the notification area” is off until you turn it on; without it, automations run only while sAInmore is open.
  • Translate and Summarise (parts of DeepDive) send your text to the model you pick; with Ollama or LM Studio nothing leaves your PC. Summarise downloads the page whose address you type, from your PC: https only, without cookies or sign-ins, never an AI service or an address on your local network. It then sends the page’s text to the model you pick. Summarise on a web page in the Workspace sends the text of the page you have open. Only their token counts (no text) are kept on your PC, for Usage, and only when Save history is on.
  • Updates: the first time you start the app, its Welcome screen asks whether it may check GitHub for new versions. Until you say yes (or turn the check on in Settings → About), nothing is checked by itself; if you haven’t answered after a few days, it asks once more in a notice. After a yes, the app asks GitHub whether a newer version exists when it starts and every 6 hours while it runs. GitHub sees your IP address and the app’s name and version. If you were already using sAInmore before the question was added, your setting stays as it was. A new version is downloaded from GitHub only when you click Update now. You can turn the check off in Settings → About; Check for updates in the Menu asks GitHub the same question only when you click it.
  • Feedback is only sent if you choose to. The app prepares an email or a file, and you decide what goes in it (a screenshot, the error log, system details).
  • Streaming sites: films on listed streaming sites open in Edge or Chrome (or your default browser if you have neither), which handle them as they do any browsing. Your sign-ins and history for that window stay on this PC, in a browser profile inside sAInmore’s data folder, apart from your normal browser. They aren’t in backups or Sync, and uninstalling sAInmore leaves them there. Forget streaming sign-ins removes them.
  • Sync between your PCs is off unless you set it up. It writes encrypted files to a folder you choose, such as one synced by OneDrive. Your passphrase isn’t stored in that folder.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It doesn’t read the AI websites’ pages, type into them or send messages on them for you.
  • It doesn’t scrape websites. Automated comparisons go through the providers’ official APIs or a server you add yourself, and Summarise reads only a page you ask it to.
  • It doesn’t sell or share data. There’s nothing for us to share, because we don’t receive any.

Costs and subscriptions

Website subscriptions (such as ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro) and API billing are separate. API use is billed to you by each provider.
